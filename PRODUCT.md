# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

B2B owners evaluating cold-email sales outreach, and teams evaluating customer support on website chat and Instagram.

## Product Purpose

Help a suitable visitor understand the services, evaluate real evidence and book an introductory call.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The approved 2026-10-02 brief is a six-page website, with complete English and Italian versions. Sales replies require human approval by default. Support agents answer from supplied information and hand unresolved cases to the team through tickets. Preserve the existing form and calendar behavior, including calendar loading only after a click.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the existing FlowSync identity and design system. Plain words, sentence case and Italian with tu. The project instructions remain authoritative for the exact visual rules and permitted claims.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing anonymized campaign and shop records in `src/components/Results.jsx`, with their dates, definitions and limitations. Illustrations must be labeled. No supplied founder photograph, testimonials or new case narratives.

## Product Principles

- Give sales and support visitors a relevant path.
- Explain what happens and where a person stays involved.
- Let evidence carry the argument.
- Make booking easy without loading third-party services before the visitor chooses.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Complete keyboard and touch operation, visible focus, reduced-motion support and readable layouts from small phones to wide desktops.
